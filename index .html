About me

A little about me.

I like owning a product from end to end: designing the database, building the API, and polishing the screen a user finally touches. That is why I build complete products instead of small practice apps.

My recent work includes a deployment platform that detects a project’s framework and ships it automatically, a digital store with real payments and instant delivery, and an agency website with a 3D landing page.

Anvil Studio is a web development and cloud services agency site that I built. It lays out what a client can order, from websites and game server hosting to VPS setup, bots and SEO, with clear fixed prices, so visitors know exactly what they are paying for before they get in touch.

On the technical side it uses Next.js and Three.js for an animated 3D landing page with smooth scrolling, plus a multi-step order form that sends each request straight to email or WhatsApp. An admin panel sits behind it to manage the site.

I keep my code simple, test what I ship, and keep sharpening Java, DSA and system design. I’m open to internships, freelance work and teams where I can build, learn and own real features.
